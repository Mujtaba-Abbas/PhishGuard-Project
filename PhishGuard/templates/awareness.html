LEARN TO SPOT THE SIGNS

Think before you click.

Phishing messages are designed to look trustworthy. Use these checks to help protect your information.

↗
01 · URLS

Check the website address

Look carefully for misspellings, unexpected subdomains, strange characters, and domains that do not match the organization.

⚡
02 · PRESSURE

Question urgent requests

Messages claiming your account will be closed or demanding immediate action may be trying to stop you from thinking carefully.

♙
03 · PRIVACY

Protect credentials

Do not send passwords, PINs, or one-time passcodes by email or message. Use official account pages to verify requests.

✉
04 · SENDER

Verify the sender

Check the complete sender address and confirm unusual requests using a known official contact method.

⌁
05 · LINKS

Be cautious with links

Hover over links on a computer to inspect their destination. On mobile, avoid opening unexpected links and navigate directly to official sites.

✓
06 · REPORT

Report suspicious content

Use your email provider's phishing report option or contact your organization's IT/security team.

Remember: A low score does not prove content is safe. PhishGuard uses predefined educational rules and is not a replacement for professional security tools.
